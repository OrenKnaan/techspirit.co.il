Questions people usually ask

Most clients pay between ₪2,500 and ₪6,500 for a full website, and payments can be split.

Every project is different - it depends on how many pages you need, what you want to include and your specific needs. After a short intro call I prepare a clear price quote, with no surprises.

A full website is usually ready within three weeks of the first call.

A landing page - sometimes a week to ten days. The pace depends on you too: the faster you respond and the sooner you bring content and images, the faster everything moves.

Not at all - that's actually the reason we work well together.

The whole technical side is on me, from the domain to security. After the website goes live you get a short tutorial video so you can update content yourself whenever you want - and if you don't want to, that's fine too.

It depends - there's no generic answer, I look at each case on its own merits.

I look at what you have and tell you straight what I recommend - even if that means there's no point investing right now. Sometimes a few focused changes do more good than rebuilding. Sometimes the opposite. We'll check together.

I don't let go - there's support for the first two weeks.

You get two weeks of support - questions, small fixes, whatever you need. Want to continue with ongoing maintenance? That's an option. Prefer to be fully independent? I prepare you for that from day one.

Wix is good for trying things out; a professional website is built around your goal.

With Wix you'll spend money every month, run into limitations, and the result usually looks like a template. A professional website is built around your story, is faster, and isn't "locked" into any platform. Once you have a website like that, you'll feel the difference.

The website doesn't bring clients - it converts interest into contact.

Clients arrive through Google, recommendations or social media. The website is what turns "interesting" into "I want to book". That's why design, content and SEO work together - one without the others doesn't do much.

Yes - and designed for mobile first.

More than 70% of browsing in Israel is on mobile. Every website I build is planned from the start for small screens - the design, the speed and the UX.

It's possible - writing is part of what I bring to the table.

You can bring raw content and I'll design it, we can write together, or I can write everything based on a conversation between us. I don't copy-paste generic wording - the writing is tailored to your voice.

Yes, but not necessarily - it depends on what suits you.

WordPress is a great fit for blogs, stores and websites that are updated often. For small spiritual businesses I sometimes recommend static HTML - fast, cheap to maintain, and more secure. We'll decide together what the right tool is.

Maintenance costs ₪20 a month.

Hosting, backups, security updates, and availability for questions and small fixes. If you want ongoing content updates - that's possible too, in an expanded plan. I lay out the options clearly and don't push what you don't need.

No - I take care of all that if you like.

I can take hosting and a domain in your name, and you simply receive a working website. Or you can hold everything in your own name while I only build - whatever is comfortable for you.

SEO = showing up on Google when someone searches for what you offer.

Every website I build includes basic SEO - proper structure, headings, descriptions, speed. Advanced SEO with content writing and link building is a separate service. Not everyone needs it, and I'll tell you honestly if it's relevant for you.

Most spiritual businesses in Israel do just fine with Hebrew only.

If some of your clients speak English - an English version can be added. But it doubles the cost of writing and maintenance. I recommend starting with Hebrew, and seeing whether there's real demand for English.

I understand the field from the inside - not just technically.

I've worked with coaches, therapists, workshop facilitators and spiritual centers. I know your challenge isn't "putting information on the internet" - it's building trust with people who are looking for resonance. The websites I build don't look like insurance and they don't look like a store - they look like you.
