How Much Does a Landing Page Cost? 2026 Price Guide

June 13, 20267 min readOren Knaan

A landing page sounds simple: one page, one CTA, a few blocks of text. So why is there a 10,000 shekel gap between one quote and another? And why do pages that cost 500 shekels not convert, while pages that cost 6,000 do? Here's the explanation.

5 things worth knowing before you order a landing page

  • A landing page is not a small website. It's a marketing tool with one goal: to move a visitor to action. Anything that doesn't contribute to that action hurts it.
  • The most common reason a landing page fails is the main headline, not the design.
  • Professional copy is usually 40-60% of the total price, and it's the factor that affects conversion the most.
  • A simple landing page with a sharp message converts better than a designed page with a confused message.
  • For businesses just starting out, a landing page is usually the right step before a full website.

A landing page is not a small website

This distinction matters. A website is a digital space with several pages, a navigation menu, and a blog. It's meant to build trust over time and bring organic traffic from Google.

A landing page is a single page with one specific goal. No menu. No outgoing links. No distractions. Every element on the page is meant to get the visitor to take one action: leave their details, buy, sign up.

A landing page that asks a visitor for more than one action isn't a landing page. It's a page of confusion.

What determines the price of a landing page?

1. Copy: the factor that affects conversion the most

Copy is the marketing text of the page. The headline, the description of benefits, the CTA. An experienced copywriter charges 2,000-6,000 shekels for a landing page. This is the amount that surprises people the most, and rightly so.

But one good headline can double the conversion rate without changing a line of code. A bad headline ruins good design. That's why copy is usually what separates a page that works from one that doesn't.

2. Design: template vs. custom

A ready-made landing page template looks professional for most needs. Custom design adds 1,500-5,000 shekels. For most landing pages, it isn't necessary. What is necessary is that the design doesn't distract from the CTA.

3. Page length

A short page for a simple lead (name, email, submit) is different from a long sales page that breaks down objections and explains the product in depth. A long page requires more copy, more design, and usually more thought. It costs more because it's worth more when built right.

4. Integrations

Connecting to a mailing list (Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign), a CRM, or a lead management system adds 500-1,500 shekels. The Facebook pixel and Google Analytics are basic and should be included on any page used for a campaign.

2026 landing page price list

By page type

  • Landing page with design based on existing branding (no copy): ₪1,500-2,500
  • Landing page with custom design (no copy): ₪2,500-4,500
  • Landing page with professional copy: ₪4,000-7,000

Related costs to take into account

  • Dedicated domain: ₪95 per year (not required)
  • Hosting: ₪600-900 per year (cheaper options exist, but then I can't provide a maintenance service)
  • Ongoing maintenance: ₪120 per year

Note: the prices above do not include search engine optimization (SEO/AEO). For landing pages meant to stay active over time and attract clients organically from Google, it's recommended to add an SEO layer, which is built separately and priced by scope.

A landing page that doesn't convert is almost always a headline problem, not a design problem. One good headline can double the conversion rate without changing a line of code.

When is a landing page right and when is a full website?

The simple answer: they're two different tools for two different goals. Sometimes you need both.

When a landing page is the right choice

  • You're running a paid campaign (Google Ads, Facebook) and want a page focused on one goal
  • You're just starting out and want to test your message before investing in a full website
  • You're launching a new service and want to gather early responses
  • You have one specific service you want to promote separately

When a full website is the right choice

  • You want to rank on Google for organic search terms over time
  • You have several services that need separate explanation
  • Your clients research before they buy and want to get to know you in depth
  • You want to post content (a blog) that builds authority in your field

For most small businesses just starting out, the landing page is the right step first. After you validate that there's a market and that the message works, you build a full website with SEO.

What to ask before ordering a landing page

These are the questions that separate an order that ends with a page that works from one that ends with a design that looks good but doesn't bring leads.

Questions worth asking every builder

  • Is copy included? If not, who writes it and at what price?
  • Where do the leads go? Email? CRM? Google Sheets?
  • Is the page on my domain? If you end the engagement with the website builder, does the page stay with you?
  • How many rounds of revisions are included? What happens if you want to change something after it goes live?
  • Are the Facebook pixel and Google Analytics set up? Without them you can't know what works.
  • Can the builder show landing pages they built that convert? This is where the talk ends and the proof begins.

A builder who can't answer some of these questions before you sign, probably didn't come with the right experience for your project.

Questions and answers about landing page cost

How much does a landing page cost for a business in Israel?

A landing page with design based on existing branding costs 1,500-2,500 shekels. A page with custom design costs 2,500-4,500 shekels. With professional copy, 4,000-7,000 shekels.

The big gap between offers comes mainly from persuasive copywriting. Professional copy is usually what separates a page that converts from one that doesn't. Before comparing prices, ask whether the copy is included, because two offers for a "landing page" can include very different things.

What's the difference between a landing page and a website?

A website is a digital space with several pages meant to build trust over time. A landing page is one page with one goal.

A landing page has no navigation menu, no outgoing links, and no distractions. Every element on the page is meant to get the visitor to take one specific action. A good website builds authority and brings traffic from Google over time. A good landing page converts paid traffic into action here and now.

When is a landing page right and when is a full website?

A landing page suits a specific paid campaign or a business that wants to test the market. A full website suits organic ranking on Google and several services.

For businesses just starting out, a landing page is the right first step. After you validate that there's a market and that the message works, you build a full website with SEO. Not the other way around. Building fast with slow validation is better than building slowly with no validation at all.

How much does a landing page with copywriting cost?

A landing page with professional copy costs 4,000-7,000 shekels. The copy alone usually costs 2,000-6,000 shekels.

It sounds like a lot, but a page that converts well will return the investment with the first leads. An experienced copywriter who understands persuasion and sales isn't a luxury, they're the difference between a page that works and a page that sits there and does nothing.

Can I build a landing page myself?

Yes. Platforms like Wix and Leadpages allow self-building. The technical build is the easy part.

The real challenge is the copy: writing a headline that makes a person keep reading, explaining value in a way that speaks to the right audience, and phrasing a CTA that drives action. People who build landing pages themselves and get disappointed usually designed well but wrote weakly. Check your headline before you check the design.

How long does it take to build a landing page?

A landing page with design based on existing branding takes 3-7 working days. A page with custom design and content takes 2-3 weeks.

The timeline depends mainly on how quickly the client provides information, images, and approvals. In most projects the side that's late isn't the builder. If you need to make a specific campaign date, say so in advance and account for it in the brief.

Why isn't my landing page converting leads?

The most common reasons, in order: a headline that doesn't speak to the audience's pain, an unclear CTA, and too many goals on one page.

Before investing in a new design, check: does the main headline talk about the client's problem and not your solution? Is there one clear CTA? Are there testimonials that strengthen trust? Does the page load fast? In most cases the problem is in the content, not the graphics.

What should a landing page that converts include?

A main headline that speaks to the audience's problem, a subheadline that explains the solution, benefits focused on results, social proof, and one clear CTA.

What it doesn't need: a navigation menu, links leaving the page, and more than one topic. Page speed directly affects the bounce rate and the Quality Score on Google. A page that loads after 4 seconds loses half its visitors before they've read a word.

What's the difference between a landing page and a sales page?

A landing page is short and aimed at a lead. A sales page is long and aimed at a direct purchase.

A sales page breaks down objections, explains the product in depth, and guides the reader to a decision. It can include 2,000-5,000 words. It costs more to build because the copy is more complex and in-depth. For courses, programs, and high-priced digital products, a sales page is the right investment.

How much does a WordPress landing page cost?

A WordPress landing page costs 1,500-5,000 shekels for development, in addition to hosting and a domain.

The advantage of WordPress is flexibility: you can change the design, add pages, and integrate SEO tools. The disadvantage is that it requires ongoing maintenance. For a one-off landing page for a campaign, simpler SaaS platforms exist, although the page doesn't belong to you at the end of the contract.

How much does a landing page for Google Ads cost?

The build costs the same, but a page built right for Google Ads lowers the cost per click by 20-40%.

Google's Quality Score measures the relevance between the ad, the search term, and the landing page. A high score lowers the cost per click. A page built specifically for the terms you advertise on, with good speed and a CTA matched to the ad, will pay back the cost of the build in advertising budget savings.

What do you ask before ordering a landing page?

Five questions: is copy included? Where do the leads go? Is the page on my domain? How many revisions are included? And can the builder show pages that convert?

A builder who can't answer all of them before you sign probably hasn't built many pages that convert. A question that's sometimes forgotten: who is responsible for the page if the builder isn't available a year from now? The page should belong to you and be open to changes without depending on one person.

What's the difference between a cheap and an expensive landing page?

A cheap page is usually a template with your text. An expensive page includes audience research, tailored copy, and design that serves the message.

The most significant difference is in the copy. A page written by an experienced copywriter converts on average 2-3 times better than a page with generic text, even if the design is identical. That's why "cheap" and "expensive" aren't precise. The right question is: what is the ROI cost over the life of the campaign?

Do I need a domain for a landing page?

Not required, but worth it. A page on a subdomain of your own looks more professional and doesn't depend on a third-party platform.

A .co.il domain costs 95 shekels a year, which is almost free. If the page is part of a one-off campaign, you can do without a dedicated domain. If it's a page that runs over time, it should sit on your address and not someone else's. A subdomain like offer.yourname.co.il looks trustworthy, is quick to read, and is easy to change if needed.
