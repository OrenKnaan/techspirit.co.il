Questions and answers about website hosting

What is website hosting?

Website hosting is a server where your website's code sits, and when someone visits your address it returns the pages to them.

Without hosting there's no website, just as without a physical location you can't open a store. Most business owners don't think about it until something breaks, and that's exactly the problem. They choose hosting by price and then discover the support doesn't exist when they need it.

How much does website hosting cost in Israel?

Basic hosting starts at 20-40 shekels a month; managed WordPress hosting in Israel costs 80-200 shekels a month.

uPress is at the high end and in my opinion it's worth it. The difference between cheap and quality hosting is 50-150 shekels a month. Added up over a year, that's 600-1,800 shekels. One hour of an IT person fixing a problem usually costs more than that.

Does website hosting affect SEO?

Yes, directly. Loading speed is a Google ranking factor and a slow server lowers your Core Web Vitals.

Google prefers websites that load in under 3 seconds, and a slow server alone can add 1-2 seconds to the load time. Beyond that, a website that goes down from time to time gets a low uptime score and loses ranking. Good hosting is basic SEO infrastructure that affects everything else.

What's the difference between website hosting and a domain?

The domain is your address on the web, the hosting is the server the website sits on - two completely separate services.

You can buy a domain from one place and hosting from another. Some providers sell them together. The most important thing is knowing who holds each of them and where the login details are, because if the builder holds them and you part ways, there's a problem.

What is managed WordPress hosting?

A server specially tuned for WordPress with automatic backups, security updates, and a dedicated management interface.

Compared with general hosting where you manage everything yourself. The practical difference: with managed hosting you don't need to know how to run a manual backup, how to update WordPress core safely, or how to manage an SSL certificate. All of this happens automatically, which frees up time for the business itself.

Which is better, hosting in Israel or abroad?

For a website aimed at an Israeli audience, hosting in Israel is faster because the physical distance between the server and the visitor affects speed.

A server in Israel can save 200-400 milliseconds on every request, which adds up to a faster experience. Beyond speed, there's the advantage of support in Hebrew during Israeli hours, answers to questions without a language barrier, and availability during the hours you work.

What should I look for in a hosting service?

Four critical things: speed, reliability (uptime above 99.9%), accessible technical support, and automatic backups.

All other features are a bonus. Before choosing, it's worth checking real reviews of the support, not just the price. Because when something breaks, the only question that matters is how fast someone will answer.

Is SSL included in hosting service?

In most modern hosting services yes; SSL makes the address start with https and the browser display a padlock.

Without SSL, Google flags the website as not secure and your ranking drops. If the hosting service doesn't include free SSL, you can add a certificate through Let's Encrypt, but that requires technical knowledge. It's better to choose a service that handles it automatically.

How many GB does a small business website need?

For most small websites, 5-10 GB is comfortably enough.

The average website of a service business with a few images and pages takes 0.5-2 GB. The problem usually isn't the volume but the speed and the support. Don't choose a service only by the amount of GB offered without looking at the other parameters.

Can you switch between hosting services?

Yes, it's all your code and content and there's nothing preventing you from switching.

A move includes transferring the website files, the database, and updating the domain settings. A good hosting service will help with the process. It usually takes 24-48 hours until the changes propagate across the network. It's worth planning the move for a weekend or a quiet period in the business.

When does cheap hosting become expensive?

When the website went down and no one answered, when it got hacked and there was no backup, when it was slow and you lost clients who left before the page loaded.

The time you waste on problems is worth much more than the monthly savings. If you earn 300 shekels an hour, one hour of trouble already costs more than half a year of more expensive hosting. It's the simple math most people don't do when they choose by price.
